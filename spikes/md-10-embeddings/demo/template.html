How to read it. Green means the question was merged into an earlier group because it scored above the first rule. Amber means it scored in between, so the board only suggests the merge and the instructor decides. Blue means a new group. Slide suggestions are hidden below a similarity of 0.45, because that is where answers stop being reliable. A question you type joins the board after the questions already asked, and is compared with all of them.

About the data. The 113 sample questions are drafts written by the team in student style, not real student questions, so accuracy on real questions will probably be lower. Slide text comes from two real lecture decks and is shortened here. Your own questions are embedded by the local server and are not sent anywhere else.
